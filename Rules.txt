🎮 Zone Runner – Rules
🟩 Survive & Run: Keep moving forward and go as far as possible.
❤️ 3 Lives Only: You lose a life when you hit obstacles.
⚠️ Avoid Obstacles: Spikes, enemies, lasers, drones, asteroids, and more.
🌈 Zone Changes: Each zone has unique physics:
Green → Normal
Blue → Slippery ice
Yellow → Inverted gravity
Gray → Zero gravity
🎯 Adapt Fast: Controls and movement feel change in every zone.
💀 Game Over: When all lives are lost.
🏆 Goal: Beat your best distance score.
